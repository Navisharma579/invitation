> **सोमवार, 14 दिसंबर, 2026 · जयपुर, राजस्थान**

A luxury digital wedding invitation website for a traditional Rajasthani royal wedding, featuring Jaipur palace haveli aesthetics, interactive 3D royal wax seal opening envelope, shehnai instrumental music, countdown timer, event schedule, Google Maps venue navigation, and WhatsApp RSVP.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Royal Envelope Opening Experience**:
  - Ambient Jaipur palace haveli night courtyard with warm lanterns and lotus pool reflections.
  - Authentic 3D Royal Lac Wax Seal (शाही मुहर) in crimson lacquer with raised crown 👑 & `R&M` gold monogram.
  - Rajput scalloped arch flap with 180° opening animation.
  - Gold-bordered secret card gliding reveal (`translateY`).
- **Devotional Ganesha Blessings**: Sanskrit shloka with traditional gold artwork.
- **Wedding Events Timeline**:
  - 6 Dec 2026: Ganesh Nimantran (6:00 AM)
  - 10 Dec 2026: Godh (11:00 AM), Lagan (12:15 PM), Baan Sankadi (2:00 PM), Preetibhoj (7:00 PM onwards)
  - 13 Dec 2026: Janeyu Sanskar (8:00 AM), Chaak (2:00 PM)
  - 14 Dec 2026: Barat Nikasi (3:00 PM), Panigrahan Sanskar (Shubh Lagnanusar)
- **Venues & Google Maps**:
  - Main Venue: ॐ राधिका मैरिज गार्डन, मार्बल मंडी चौराहा, कालवाड़ रोड, जयपुर
  - Baraat Departure: श्री बालाजी बाग मैरिज गार्डन, नृसिंह विहार, डी.जी. फार्म के पास, जयपुर
- **Interactive RSVP**: Direct WhatsApp confirmation to the family (9460385222 / 7742907042).
- **Background Music**: Traditional shehnai instrumental starting at 25 seconds in with floating player controls.
- **Mobile First & Responsive**: Optimized for iOS & Android smartphones.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

Double-click `run_website.bat` or run:

```bash
cd rajasthani
node serve.js
```

Then open `http://localhost:3001` in your browser.
